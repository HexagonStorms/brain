For plfog member-facing content — the Wiki how-it-works guides, and by extension changelog notes and in-app notices — Josh wants an "explain it like I'm 14" register: concise and clear, short sentences, plain words, no filler or throat-clearing. Not overly verbose.

**Why:** the audience is hobbyist makers (woodworkers, ceramicists), not engineers. A curious teenager should get it on one read.

**How to apply:** cut lead-ins ("This is where...", "Good to know:"); collapse multi-clause sentences; prefer a short numbered list over a prose paragraph; aim roughly a third shorter than a "complete" draft. Keep every verified fact and permission caveat intact (brevity must not drop a "guild leads only" or "not confirmed until a lead approves"). Still no em dashes or standalone hyphen-dashes in anything Josh pastes verbatim — see [[feedback_no_dashes_in_copy]]. Content plan + build spec live in the worktree's docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-23-wiki-articles.md.
